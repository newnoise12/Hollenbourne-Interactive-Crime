# Weekly Unlock Schedule

*Consolidates all week-specific timing from across the design documents into one view. Firm week numbers are used where one was actually assigned; the CJS-structure quizzes (Police/CPS/Courts/Prisons) were designed with descriptive labels, not pinned to individual week numbers within the 3–7 block — flagged honestly below rather than inventing false precision.*

---

**Week 2** — Trustworthiness quiz (two-stage ranking exercise)

**Week 3** — Referencing quiz (the foundational teaching moment — general source types, not case-specific). **Evidence unlocks: victim biographies for all four deaths**, ahead of everything else — a deliberate early seed of human stakes before the investigation itself begins.

**Week 4** — Dark figure of crime quiz (real ONS data, three charts, plus a referencing-practice section reusing the Week 3 skill)

**Weeks 3–7 (exact week TBD within this block)** — CJS structure content, reusing Chris's existing lecture material, with one built activity per sub-topic:
- Police → PACE quiz
- CPS → CPS quiz
- Courts/sentencing → the two argument formation activities (council funding, force structure)
- Prisons → Prisons/reoffending quiz (real MOJ data, dual-axis chart-construction question, referencing-practice section)
- Probation → **no activity built yet — the one open gap**

**Week 8** — Crime representation (existing lecture content; no bespoke game activity built, ties to Chris's own research area)

**Week 9** — **The investigation properly begins.** Full baseline evidence bundle unlocks for all four cases simultaneously (Mason, Wooley, Porterhouse, Butt) — not staged case-by-case, since the point is spotting a pattern across all four at once:
- Policy logs, FLO logs, house-to-house canvass summaries (free)
- Missing person report, town/estate maps, homicide-rate background (free)
- Initial forensic reports, all four cases (small institutional-inertia cost — 1 point each)
- Initial interview transcripts for Burgess, Nigel, Haddad, Swayne (small institutional-inertia cost — 1 point each; these also gate the general re-interview actions)
- The full costed action economy opens up from here — see the unlock tree for the complete gating structure

**Week 10 or 11** (final call not pinned to one specific week) — **Sara Butt's follow-up forensic report releases** (the strangulation finding that supersedes "bludgeoned"). Deliberately not Week 9 — preserves the moment where this quietly undermines Ferris's own memo, which a team should get to discover mid-investigation rather than have handed to them on day one.

**Week 11, first half** — Mock CW2 assessment (graded, mirrors the real summative assessment's format and skills)

**Week 11, second half** — **The endgame.** Unassessed, competitive, offline: a short form (who/what charge/what evidence, per case), hand-graded by Chris against the rubric. No in-app mechanism needed for this part at all.
